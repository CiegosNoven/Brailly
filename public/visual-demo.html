The Harbor Museum

Explore the history of the harbor in galleries, a sculpture garden, and a waterside café. This is a controlled demonstration page, not a real museum or travel advisory.

Plan your visit

Open Tuesday–Sunday, 10 am–6 pm. Closed Mondays. Adult admission is $18; visitors under 16 enter free. The ticket includes all permanent galleries and the sculpture garden. Allow about two hours for your visit. Last gallery admission is at 5 pm, and the café closes at 5:30 pm.
